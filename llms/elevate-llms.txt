# Elevate

> AI-powered, cloud-native consumer-directed benefits administration platform for HSAs, FSAs, HRAs, and commuter, lifestyle, and family-forming accounts. Built API-first and white-label so administrators, TPAs, PEOs, brokers, financial institutions, and health plans can embed real-time account management, instant claims, and reimbursement into their own front ends. Founded 2020; backed by Anthemis, Fin Capital, Norwest Venture Partners, Greycroft, Bowery Capital, Firebolt Ventures, and SaaS Ventures. HIPAA and AICPA SOC compliant. The Elevate API is partner-gated (access via demo/contract); there is no public OpenAPI specification.

## Product
- [Benefits technology platform](https://www.elevate.ai/benefits-technology-platform): HSAs, FSAs, HRAs, commuter, lifestyle, family-forming
- [HSA software](https://www.elevate.ai/full-platform/hsa-software): modern health savings account administration
- [Benefit platforms use case](https://www.elevate.ai/use-cases/benefit-platforms): white-label, API-driven embedding for partners
